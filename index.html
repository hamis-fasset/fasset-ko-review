How this works

  1. On the left is the app in English. On the right is how it will look in Korean. Click any text on either phone to edit it.
  2. Fix the Korean in the panel on the right. Your changes show up on the phone as you type. Tick “Looks good” when a line is fine.
  3. When a screen is finished, press “Mark this screen as done”. Your work saves automatically in this browser. When you are done for the day, press “Save my edits to a file” and send us the file.

The Korean phone is a preview drawn over the English picture, so spacing is close to the real app but not exact. Text underlined in orange or red is likely too long for its space.
