How this works

  1. On the left is the app in English. On the right is how it will look in Korean. Click any text on either phone to edit it.
  2. Fix the Korean in the panel on the right. Your changes show up on the phone as you type. Tick “Looks good” when a line is fine.
  3. When a screen is finished, press “Mark this screen as done”. A secure reviewer link saves every change to the server and keeps a browser backup. Press “Submit review” when you are finished. “Download backup” is only an emergency copy.

This is one app. Sign up, log in, your details, identity check and home come from the new design; every other pictured screen is the current app as it looks today. Screens run in the order a customer meets them. “Unmapped copy” pairs English and Korean for strings that are in the two developer handoffs but are not tied to a pictured state. The Korean phone is a layout preview, so spacing is close to the real app but not pixel-perfect. If Korean may not fit, the editor panel shows a clear length warning.
